FAQs

Questions,
answered.

How do we get started?

We start with a quick chat where you share your idea, goals, and what you need. From there, I propose the right app architecture and next steps.

What if I'm not sure what I need yet?

That's completely fine. I help you figure it out. We break things down together and define what will actually work for your product.

Will I be involved in the development process?

Yes. You'll be part of key stages like scope, design, feedback, and testing. The process stays collaborative, not one-sided.

How many revisions can I request?

I include a reasonable number of revisions to make sure the final result feels right. The focus is on getting it correct, not rushing it. If there's a need for longer support, we can discuss a retainer.

Do you only build, or also help with ideas?

I do both. I help shape the idea, not just build it. The goal is to make something that works, not just something that looks good.

How long does a typical project take to complete?

Timelines depend on the scope. A focused app or AI tool usually takes 2–4 weeks, while larger products can take 2–3 months. I share a clear timeline after understanding your requirements.

Can you handle both Android and backend work?

Yes. From Android apps and UI to AI backends and cloud services, I make sure everything works together end to end.
